Our Story

From a Vision to a Global
Electric Mobility Manufacturer

AiLE was founded with a clear mission: to engineer and manufacture premium electric bikes and electric scooters that meet the demands of global brands, distributors and mobility businesses.

Over 15+ years in the electric mobility industry, we have built comprehensive capabilities across product design, engineering, prototyping, testing and mass production — covering both the Electric Bike and Electric Scooter product lines.

Today, AiLE serves clients across Europe, North America, Japan, South Korea, Australia and beyond.
